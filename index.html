SkinVisionML — Skin Cancer Detection with Class-Balanced Deep Learning

Featured Project

Developed a dermoscopic skin lesion classification pipeline for the HAM10000 dataset, addressing extreme class imbalance in multiclass medical image classification. The final system combines a ResNet-18 backbone with class-balanced cross-entropy loss, ImageNet transfer learning, and a two-stage fine-tuning strategy to improve minority-class recognition.

The final model achieved strong overall performance while substantially improving underrepresented lesion categories such as basal cell carcinoma (BCC), actinic keratoses (AKIEC), and dermatofibroma (DF), making the project especially relevant for real-world medical AI settings.

Tech: Python, PyTorch, ResNet-18, Transfer Learning, Medical Imaging, Deep Learning, Classification
